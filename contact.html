Frequently asked questions

Is CSVNow really free? +
Yes — the Free plan is free forever with no credit card required. You get access to all conversion formats, CSV Pulse, Compatibility Checker, and more. Paid plans unlock larger files, unlimited conversions, and AI tools.
Are my files private? +
On the Free plan, all processing happens in your browser — your files never reach our servers. On Pro/Business, files sent for AI features are processed in memory and deleted immediately. We never read or store your file content.
How do I cancel my subscription? +
You can cancel anytime from your account settings or by emailing us. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period. No questions asked.
Can I get a refund? +
Yes — we offer a 7-day refund policy for first-time subscribers. Email us within 7 days of your first payment and we'll process the refund promptly.
Do you offer an API? +
Yes, the Business plan includes full REST API access with 1,000 calls/day. Contact us if you need higher limits or a custom plan.
